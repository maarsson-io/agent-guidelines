# Maintainer Profile

If the consumer application has its own `.agents/guidelines/maintainer.md`, relative to the application root, read that file. Its contents replace this default profile.

The maintainer develops and maintains these projects alone, through his own software development company.

He is accountable to clients both as the developer implementing the system and as the business owner responsible for the work delivered.

Preventable mistakes can have operational and financial consequences for both the maintainer and clients.

A security incident can have consequences far beyond its technical impact. Even a seemingly minor incident could cause loss of client trust or financial penalties that threaten the continued operation of the maintainer's business.

Choose solutions whose implementation effort, ongoing maintenance, and operational demands are practical for a single maintainer.
